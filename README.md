### Installing the APK on Android

1. Open the [**latest release**](https://github.com/AlexBuildsLTS/OpusHunter-Release/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `.apk` file to download it.
3. When prompted, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (_Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps_).
4. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**.
5. Launch **OpusHunter**, explore the **Live Sandbox** or sign in with Google / Email to set up your Vault.

> [!TIP]
> **Updating from an earlier build?** Install the new APK directly over the existing app — your profile, Document Vault, Proof Link, and pipeline live securely in the cloud and stay 100% intact.

> [!NOTE]
> Android may display a standard "Play Protect" prompt for direct APK releases outside the Play Store. Tap **Install anyway** to proceed.

**Requirements:** Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) or newer · Internet connection · Optional Google or email account (or instant zero-login Sandbox).

## 🔄 How It Works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["👤 Set up profile<br/>roles, seniority, city and language"] --> B["📂 Populate Vault<br/>CV, GitHub/ZIP portfolios, diplomas"]
    B --> C["🔭 Radar scans<br/>regional and global boards"]
    C --> D["📋 4-Stage Pipeline<br/>Swipe Deck, Kanban and Matrix"]
    D --> E["🧠 AI tailors<br/>letter, vector A4 CV and Copilot"]
    E --> F["🛡️ Share Proof & Apply<br/>/v/slug + 1-Tap Email or Portal"]
```

1. **Configure your profile** — target roles, seniority levels, preferred cities (with GPS lock), and your preferred AI/CV language from **100+ supported languages**.
2. **Populate your Document Vault** — upload your CV (`.pdf` / `.docx`), import public GitHub repositories or `.zip` / Repomix archives, attach multi-channel live release links (`Google Play`, `App Store`, `Live Web`, `APK/IPA`), and upload diplomas/certificates.
3. **Run the Job Radar** — fresh listings are harvested across Swedish, Nordic, European, and global feeds, scored deterministically against your verified skills, and deduplicated via SHA-256.
4. **Triage your 4-stage pipeline** — swipe cards in the Discovery Deck, drag across the 4-stage Kanban board (`Discovered · Saved · Applied · Interview`), or filter and sort 500+ roles at 120fps in the Matrix List view.
5. **Tailor, chat & verify** — generate an evidence-grounded native-language cover letter (zero artificial word ceilings), refine a selectable 1-page vector A4 CV PDF across 6 layout architectures, or open the blue **Copilot Chat** for screening answers, recruiter replies, and interview preparation.
6. **Publish your Proof Link & apply in one tap** — share your zero-login **Verified Candidate Proof Page** (`/v/your-name`) alongside a concise 4-line direct CTO / Tech Lead message, or dispatch directly from your linked Gmail / Outlook account with CV and certification PDFs attached.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

### 🛡️ Shareable Verified Candidate Proof Pages (`/v/<slug>`) & Direct CTO Outreach Studio

- **Zero-Login Evidence Passport**: Publish an opt-in, read-only `/v/your-name` Proof Page backed strictly by your verified Vault manifests, live store/web/release links, work history, credentials, work-authorization status, 1-click contact buttons, and on-demand **1-Page Vector A4 CV (PDF)** export.
- **Interactive `<5ms` Role-Fit Evaluator for Hiring Leads**: CTOs and Engineering Managers can paste any open role description directly on your Proof Page to verify exact core stack overlap, see which shipped project proves each matched technology, and confirm honest `0% Disqualified` boundaries with zero AI hallucination and `$0` API cost.
- **Bilingual 4-Line Direct CTO Pitch Generator (`🇬🇧 EN` / `🇸🇪 SV`)**: Generates a crisp, high-converting 4-line engineering message linking directly to your live Proof Passport — built for reaching hiring managers within 24–72 hours of a fresh posting.

### 🌌 Single-Pass Skia 120fps 3D Isometric Obsidian Ambient Engine

- **Dual-Backend GPU Rendering**: Renders via a **single-pass hardware-accelerated `@shopify/react-native-skia` `<Canvas>`** on Android APK and iOS IPA (eliminating ~268 MB of offscreen software bitmaps) and browser GPU-composited SVG on Desktop & Mobile Web — zero video bloat and instant frame-1 rendering.
- **Standard (`⚡`) & Subtle Radar (`»`) Modes + Background Power Saving**: Toggle between the full isometric studio choreography (rotating radar cone, plasma pulse, hovering frosted resume tablet, retracting 3×2 card deck) and Subtle Radar mode, with automatic `AppState` suspension whenever backgrounded.

### 📋 4-Stage 120fps Kanban Board, Matrix List & Multimodal Copilot Chat

- **Streamlined 4-Stage Active Pipeline (`Discovered · Saved · Applied · Interview`)**: Responsive 4-column desktop board with smooth horizontal scroll, paired with unified single-bar stage switching on mobile viewports.
- **High-Volume `500+` Card $O(1)$ Performance**: Pre-computed canonical skill caching and $O(N)$ Schwartzian sorting keep 500+ job pipelines fluid at 120fps on both mobile and desktop.
- **1-Click Multimodal Copilot Chat**: Role-contextual assistant for screening questions, screenshot analysis, recruiter email replies, and technical interview preparation.

### 📄 AI CV Refiner, 6-Architecture Vector A4 PDF Studio & Language Pinning

- Condenses and aligns your CV around verified Vault evidence without fabricating skills or employers, with dedicated **Language Pin Slots** (`🇸🇪 SV` / `🇬🇧 EN` or any of 100+ languages).
- **6 WCAG-Verified 1-Page A4 Layout Architectures** (`1-Column Classic ATS`, `1-Column Nordic Clean`, `Executive`, `Scandinavian`, `Left Sidebar`, and `Executive Right Sidebar`) — selectable via a full obsidian-glass dropdown on Desktop Web and fast touch-swipe on Phones & iPads — exporting genuine selectable `application/pdf` files across Web, Android APK, and iOS IPA.

### 🌐 Universal 100+ Language Engine & Evidence-Grounded Cover Letters

- **Symmetrical 3×3 Quick-Access Matrix + 100+ Language Drawer**: Instant 1-tap switching across 🇸🇪 Swedish, 🇬🇧 English (UK), 🇺🇸 English (US), 🇩🇪 German, 🇳🇴 Norwegian, 🇩🇰 Danish, 🇫🇮 Finnish, 🇳🇱 Dutch, and 🇫🇷 French, plus 100+ ISO languages.
- **Verified Multi-Channel Release Links**: Cover letters naturally cite matching Vault projects and verified live URLs (`Google Play`, `App Store`, `Live Web`, `APK/IPA`, `GitHub`) with strict per-project URL ownership verification and zero artificial length ceilings.

### 🎯 Deterministic ATS Knockout Math & `Sync Radar` Hygiene

- **Deterministic Knockout Gating**: Multiplicative core-stack gating ensures roles missing mandatory primary languages or Tier-1 prerequisites score an honest **`0% Disqualified` / `MISMATCHED`** rather than inflated keyword guesses.
- **Automated & On-Demand `Sync Radar` Cleanup**: Verifies upstream listing liveness and purges expired or unbookmarked mismatched postings while strictly protecting bookmarked roles and active applications (`applied`, `interview`, `offer`).

## 🔒 Privacy, Security & Fair Access

- **Human-in-the-loop always.** Nothing is emailed, published, or marked as applied without your explicit action.
- **Strictly Opt-In Proof Pages.** Your Vault stays 100% private by default; `/v/<slug>` only exposes the snapshot you explicitly publish and can be taken offline in one tap.
- **Your email, your identity.** Gmail and Outlook connect via official OAuth 2.0 PKCE with revocable permissions.
- **Row-Level Security & Zero-Retention Closure.** Every cloud table and storage bucket enforces strict per-user Row-Level Security (`FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`), paired with a 2-step GDPR Account Closure enclave that purges all personal records and published snapshots immediately.
- **PIN & Biometric BYOK Security Vault.** Optional personal API keys are protected by hardware-backed secure storage, Face ID / Biometrics, and cryptographic PIN verification.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I try OpusHunter without creating an account?</b></summary>
<br/>
Yes! Open <a href="https://opus-hunter.vercel.app/?demo=1">opus-hunter.vercel.app/?demo=1</a> (or tap <b>LIVE SANDBOX</b> on the welcome screen) to launch the full multi-persona interactive workspace immediately. You can run live Swedish Platsbanken sweeps, switch between Software, Data/ML, and Product/UX personas, test the 4-stage Kanban pipeline, generate cover letters, and export real 1-Page Vector A4 PDFs with zero login.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is a Verified Candidate Proof Page (<code>/v/&lt;slug&gt;</code>)?</b></summary>
<br/>
From <b>Settings → Verified Proof Link</b>, you can publish an opt-in, zero-login Evidence Passport (for example, <a href="https://opus-hunter.vercel.app/v/erik-lindqvist">opus-hunter.vercel.app/v/erik-lindqvist</a>) alongside a ready-to-send 4-line direct CTO / Tech Lead pitch in English or Swedish. Hiring leads who click your link see your physically verified skills, live store/web/repo links, a <code>&lt;5ms</code> interactive Role-Fit Tester, and your 1-Page Vector A4 CV PDF without signing in — and you can unpublish it in one tap anytime.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is OpusHunter free? What are the limits?</b></summary>
<br/>
The Live Sandbox and Verified Candidate Proof Pages are completely free. Authenticated accounts include generous AI generation allowances while tier limits are under active evaluation, and power users can also connect personal API keys via the PIN & Biometric encrypted BYOK Vault.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it apply to jobs or publish my profile without asking me?</b></summary>
<br/>
Never. Every email dispatch, application submission, and Proof Page publication is 100% opt-in and requires your explicit action.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the AI make things up about me?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. Cover letters, refined CVs, Verified Proof Pages, and Copilot answers are strictly grounded in your verified Vault evidence (uploaded CVs, repositories, project archives, licenses, and diplomas). When a requirement is missing from your Vault, OpusHunter states that honestly rather than inventing claims.
</details>
