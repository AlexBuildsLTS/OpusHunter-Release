### Installing the APK on Android

1. Open the [**latest release**](https://github.com/AlexBuildsLTS/OpusHunter-Release/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `.apk` file to download it.
3. When prompted, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (_Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps_).
4. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**.
5. Launch **OpusHunter**, sign in with Google or email, and complete your profile.

> [!TIP]
> **Updating from an earlier build?** Install the new APK directly over the existing app — your profile, Document Vault, and pipeline live in Supabase cloud and stay 100% intact.

> [!NOTE]
> Android may display a standard "Play Protect" prompt for direct APK releases outside the Play Store. Tap **Install anyway** to proceed.

**Requirements:** Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) or newer · Internet connection · A Google or email account.

## 🔄 How It Works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["👤 Set up profile<br/>roles, seniority, city and language"] --> B["📂 Populate Vault<br/>CV, GitHub/ZIP portfolios, diplomas"]
    B --> C["🔭 Radar scans<br/>regional and global boards"]
    C --> D["📋 4-Stage Pipeline<br/>Swipe Deck, Kanban and Matrix"]
    D --> E["🧠 AI tailors<br/>letter, vector A4 CV and Copilot Chat"]
    E --> F["⚡ 1-Tap Apply<br/>Direct Email or Portal Dispatch"]
    F --> G["📈 Track outcomes"]
```

1. **Configure your profile** — target roles (via dynamic `BalancedChipGrid` recommendations), seniority levels, preferred cities (with GPS lock), and your preferred AI/CV language from **100+ supported languages**.
2. **Populate your Document Vault** — upload your CV (`.pdf` / `.docx`), import public GitHub repositories (`lib/githubRepoSync.ts`) or `.zip` / Repomix XML archives, attach multi-channel live release links (`Play Store`, `App Store`, `Web`, `APK/IPA`), and upload diplomas/certificates.
3. **Run the Job Radar** — fresh listings are harvested across Swedish, Nordic, European, and global feeds, scored deterministically against your verified skills, and deduplicated via SHA-256.
4. **Triage your 4-stage pipeline** — swipe cards in the Discovery Deck, drag across the 4-stage Kanban board (`Discovered · Saved · Applied · Interview`), or filter and sort 500+ roles at 120fps in the Matrix List view.
5. **Tailor, chat & verify** — generate an evidence-grounded native-language cover letter (zero artificial word or token caps), refine a selectable vector A4 CV PDF across 4 templates, or open the blue **Copilot Chat** (`InterviewChatModal.tsx`) for screening answers, recruiter replies, and interview preparation.
6. **Apply in one tap** — send directly via your linked Gmail or Outlook account (`multipart/alternative` HTML + plain text with clickable portfolio links, attached primary CV PDF, and verified certification PDFs), or launch the employer portal with your letter copied to clipboard.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

### 🌌 Procedural 120fps 3D Isometric Obsidian Ambient Engine (`components/shared/AnimatedBackground.tsx`)

- **100% Pure-Code Vector SVG + UI-Thread Reanimated 3D Scene**: Zero external video dependencies and instant frame-1 rendering across Desktop Web, Mobile Web, Android APK, and iOS IPA.
- **Standard Mode (`⚡` — Default)**: Full 7.8s isometric studio choreography featuring a matte charcoal-obsidian floor with concentric cyan-emerald telemetry ellipses, a continuous 120fps rotating isometric radar sweep cone, an expanding electric-cyan plasma energy ring, a hovering beveled frosted glass resume tablet with glowing circular progress ring and diagonal cyan laser scan, and an upper-right 3×2 frosted glass card deck whose front row retracts during the scan pulse.
- **Subtle Radar Mode (`»` in Settings Hub)**: Calmer ambient option that keeps the exact same matte obsidian background, concentric isometric rings, and rotating radar sweep while omitting the hovering frosted glass cards and laser scan.
- **Edge-to-Edge Transparent Mobile Top Scroll (`PageContainer.tsx`, `SafeAreaWrapper.tsx`)**: Delegates top floating-header clearance (`FloatingHeaderTopInsetContext`) directly into each screen's `<ScrollView contentContainerStyle>` so scrolling cards glide all the way to the top edge of the display (`y = 0`) behind the floating `LuminousSpinner` mascot and profile avatar with zero horizontal clipping seam.

### 📋 4-Stage 120fps Kanban Board, Matrix List & Copilot Chat (`pipeline.tsx`, `KanbanBoard.tsx`, `InterviewChatModal.tsx`)

- **Streamlined 4-Stage Active Pipeline (`Discovered · Saved · Applied · Interview`)**: Responsive 4-column desktop board that fits cleanly side-by-side with smooth horizontal Web scroll (`overflowX: 'auto'`, `scrollBehavior: 'smooth'`), paired with unified single-bar stage switching on Phone/APK driven directly by the top frosted stat pills (`Total Pipeline`, `Discovered`, `Saved`, `Applied`, `Interview`).
- **High-Volume `500+` Card $O(1)$ Performance Engine**: Shares pre-computed `candidateSkillSet` and $O(1)$ `alignmentByJobId` across Kanban cards and Matrix rows, memoizes per-card tech-chip matches, stabilizes status mutations via `applicationsRef`, and sorts 500+ listings via an $O(N)$ Schwartzian transform.
- **Drag-Click Suppression & 1-Click Blue Copilot Chat**: Dual drag-click suppression ensures dragging or reordering cards never triggers the card detail modal; both Kanban cards and Matrix List rows feature a 1-click electric-blue **Copilot Chat** button (`MessageSquareReply`) and **`Move to Bookmark`** protection (`TagPlus`).

### 📄 AI CV Refiner, 4-Template Vector A4 PDF Studio & Language Pinning (`generateCvHtml.ts`, `generateAtsPdf.ts`, `InlineCvTailorStudio.tsx`)

- Rewrites and condenses your CV around verified vault evidence without fabricating skills or employers, with 2 dedicated **Language Pin Slots** (`SV` / `EN` or any of 100+ languages).
- Choose between **4 executive A4 templates** with genuine cross-platform vector PDF export (`application/pdf` via `pdf-lib` & `expo-print` across Web, Android APK, and iOS IPA):
  1. **Modern Executive** (2-column calibrated navy sidebar, circular photo ring, categorized skill badges)
  2. **Nordic Clean Slate** (Scandinavian 1-column slate banner, 2×2 skill grid, framed project cards)
  3. **Technical Systems** (High-density 1-column engineering layout with monospace stack tags)
  4. **Clean Executive ATS** (Strict linear 1-column parser-first flow with crisp horizontal dividers)

### 🌐 Universal 100+ Language Engine & Symmetrical 3×3 Quick-Access Selector (`candidateLanguages.ts`)

- **Symmetrical 3×3 Quick-Access Matrix**: Instant 1-tap switching across your 9 primary regional languages (`profile.tsx` & `cover-letter/[id].tsx`):
  - 🇸🇪 **Svenska (Swedish)** · 🇬🇧 **English (UK)** · 🇺🇸 **English (US)**
  - 🇩🇪 **Deutsch (German)** · 🇳🇴 **Norsk (Norwegian)** · 🇩🇰 **Dansk (Danish)**
  - 🇫🇮 **Suomi (Finnish)** · 🇳🇱 **Nederlands (Dutch)** · 🇫🇷 **Français (French)**
- **1-Tap 100+ Language Search Drawer**: Instant, search-indexed catalog of 100+ ISO-standard languages with dialect detection, culturally authentic sign-offs, and ATS technical token retention.

### 🧠 Evidence-Grounded AI Cover Letters & Multi-Channel Release Links (`generate-cover-letter`, `projectEvidence.ts`)

- **Your real work leads the story**: Ranks your uploaded projects, direct GitHub repo imports, work history, and diplomas against each job ad with **zero artificial word or token limits**.
- **Verified Multi-Channel Release Proof**: Automatically cites and verifies per-project live distribution links (`Play Store`, `App Store`, `Web`, `APK/IPA Package`, and GitHub repo URLs) with strict anti-cross-contamination ownership checks (`projectReleaseUrlMap`).
- **Three distinct strategies & five formality profiles**: Switch between _ATS Keyword Mirroring_, _Achievement Amplifier_, and _Insider Narrative_ across _Professional_, _Formal Corporate_, _Technical Deep-Dive_, _Executive Brief_, or _Storytelling_ tones.

### 🎯 Deterministic ATS Knockout Math, Red `MISMATCHED` Badges & `Sync Radar` Auto-Purge (`atsScoring.ts`, `radarSearch.ts`, `purge-stale-jobs`)

- **Deterministic Knockout Gating**: `60%` verified skill match + `25%` structured requirements + `15%` articulation, with multiplicative title/seniority gating (`gated_score = raw_score * tech_ratio`). When required core criteria exist and candidate overlap is zero, total score is `0` and displays a crisp red **`MISMATCHED`** badge with `ShieldAlert` (never clamping to a false `1% ATS`).
- **Automated & On-Demand `Sync Radar` Hygiene**: Verifies upstream liveness across JobTech (`GET /ad/{id}`), The Hub (`/jobs/single/{id}`), SweClockers, and employer web pages, and automatically purges dead, out-of-market, or non-bookmarked `0%` `MISMATCHED` listings while strictly protecting `TagPlus`-bookmarked roles and active applications (`applied`, `interview`, `offer`).

## 🔒 Privacy, Control & Fair Access

- **Human-in-the-loop always.** Nothing is emailed or marked as applied without your explicit action.
- **Your email, your identity.** Gmail and Outlook are linked via official OAuth 2.0 PKCE (`oauth-link-email`) with automatic token health checks (`check-email-account`).
- **Row-Level Security (RLS) & Hardened RPCs.** Every PostgreSQL table and storage bucket enforces `auth.uid() = user_id` isolation, and all `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs (`recalculate_user_skills`, `delete_my_account`) pin `SET search_path = public`.
- **PIN & Biometric BYOK Security Vault.** Store personal Gemini, RapidAPI, or Hunter.io keys protected by hardware-backed secure storage, Face ID / Biometrics, and Argon2/Bcrypt PIN verification.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is OpusHunter free? What are the limits?</b></summary>
<br/>
The core app is open to test with generous AI generation allowances. Tier limits for member and premium tiers are actively under evaluation and will not be finalized until full platform stability is achieved. Administrator accounts operate with zero limits. Power users can attach their personal Gemini, RapidAPI, or Hunter.io keys via the PIN & Biometric encrypted BYOK vault.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do Sync Radar and the 12-hour listing cleanup work?</b></summary>
<br/>
OpusHunter runs an automated background hygiene routine every 12 hours (and on-demand whenever you tap <b>Sync Radar</b> in the Pipeline) that verifies live status against upstream providers (JobTech <code>GET /ad/{id}</code>, The Hub <code>/jobs/single/{id}</code>, and direct HTTP probes for 404/410) and automatically purges dead, out-of-market, or non-bookmarked <code>0%</code> <b>MISMATCHED</b> listings. <b>Crucially, active applications (<code>applied</code>, <code>interview</code>, <code>offer</code>), roles with generated cover letters, and any listing pinned with the amber <code>TagPlus</code> bookmark lock (<code>Move to Bookmark</code>) are strictly immune and permanently preserved.</b>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which languages can it write in? How does Quick Access work?</b></summary>
<br/>
Over 100+ native languages are supported via the Google Gemini cascade, with culturally appropriate greetings and formal sign-offs. In your profile and letter editor, a symmetrical 3×3 quick-access matrix gives you instant 1-tap switching between your 9 primary languages (🇸🇪 Swedish, 🇬🇧 English UK, 🇺🇸 English US, 🇩🇪 German, 🇳🇴 Norwegian, 🇩🇰 Danish, 🇫🇮 Finnish, 🇳🇱 Dutch, 🇫🇷 French). A top-right corner switcher opens the full searchable 100+ language drawer with instant cloud sync.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it apply to jobs without asking me?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. Controlled automation, not blind bots. Every application and email requires your explicit confirmation. OpusHunter prepares everything — you review and approve.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the AI make things up about me?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. Cover letters, refined CVs, and Copilot Chat answers are strictly grounded in your verified evidence ledger (CV, uploaded projects, GitHub repositories, portfolios, trade licenses, and diplomas). If it isn't in your vault, it isn't in your application.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I get the latest Android APK or use the Web App?</b></summary>
<br/>
OpusHunter <code>v2.5.7</code> (<code>Build 257</code>, Android SDK API 36) is available right now as a signed Android APK on the official <a href="https://github.com/AlexBuildsLTS/OpusHunter-Release/releases/tag/v2.5.7">GitHub Releases</a> page and live on Desktop & Mobile Web at <a href="https://opus-hunter.vercel.app/">opus-hunter.vercel.app</a> while Google Play Console internal testing progresses toward public Store promotion.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is OpusHunter only for software developers?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. OpusHunter is built for every profession: builders/construction, legal, finance, healthcare, education, design, engineering, and trades. While software developers can import GitHub repositories, code archives, and Repomix XML, any professional can upload CVs, portfolios, diplomas, and credentials.
