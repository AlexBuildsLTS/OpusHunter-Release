### Installing the APK on Android

1. Open the [**latest release**](https://github.com/AlexBuildsLTS/OpusHunter-Release/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Under **Assets**, tap the `.apk` file to download it (`OpusHunter-v1.6.4-beta.apk`).
3. When prompted, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (_Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps_).
4. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**.
5. Launch **OpusHunter**, sign in with Google or email, and complete your profile.

> [!TIP]
> **Updating from an earlier version?** Install the new APK directly over the existing app — your profile, Document Vault, and pipeline live in Supabase cloud and stay 100% intact.

> [!NOTE]
> Android may display a standard "Play Protect" prompt for direct APK releases outside the Play Store. Tap **Install anyway** to proceed.

**Requirements:** Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) or newer · Internet connection · A Google or email account.

## 🔄 How It Works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["👤 Set up profile<br/>roles, seniority, city and language"] --> B["📂 Upload to Vault<br/>CV, portfolio archives, diplomas"]
    B --> C["🔭 Radar scans<br/>regional and global boards"]
    C --> D["📋 Pipeline<br/>Swipe Deck, Kanban and Matrix"]
    D --> E["🧠 AI tailors<br/>cover letter, 1-page CV and Q and A"]
    E --> F["⚡ 1-Tap Apply<br/>Direct Email via Gmail or Outlook"]
    F --> G["📈 Track outcomes"]
```

1. **Configure your profile** — target roles, seniority levels, preferred cities (with GPS lock), and your preferred AI/CV language from **100+ supported languages**.
2. **Populate your Document Vault** — upload your CV (`.pdf` / `.docx`), project or portfolio archives (`.zip` / Repomix XML), and diplomas/certificates. OpusHunter indexes every verified fact.
3. **Run the Job Radar** — fresh listings are harvested, translated across your target search languages, and deduplicated via SHA-256.
4. **Triage your pipeline** — swipe cards in the Discovery Deck, drag across Kanban columns, or filter in the Matrix view.
5. **Tailor & verify** — generate a native-language cover letter with a deterministic ATS score, refine a 1-page CV PDF, or draft answers to employer screening questions.
6. **Apply in one tap** — send directly via your linked Gmail or Outlook account when a hiring email address is found, complete with your tailored cover letter and attached CV PDF.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

### 🎯 Anchored Card Actions & 1-Tap "View Listing" Button (`SwipeDeck.tsx`)

- **Anchored Bottom Section**: Wrapped card bottom actions in `cardBottomSection` with `marginTop: "auto"`, guaranteeing rock-solid, symmetrical button placement on Android APK, iOS, and Web regardless of listing description height.
- **Direct 1-Tap Listing Access**: Decoupled the **"View Listing"** button from description length constraints so any listing with a valid source URL provides an immediate 1-tap link styled with the amber `Ad` icon.
- **Symmetrical Specification Trigger**: Cleanly partitioned the **"View Specifications & Strategy"** trigger with the amber `SendToBack` icon and calibrated 48px touch targets.

### 🏛️ Twin Flagship Architectural Hierarchy (`projectEvidence.ts`)

- **6-Layer Fullstack & BaaS Synthesis**: Evaluates codebases across 6 architectural layers (UI, Navigation, State, Backend & Edge Services, Database/RLS, DevOps/CI-CD). Recognizes Supabase RLS and Deno Edge Services, elevating OpusHunter alongside VeraxAI into verified twin flagship status.
- **Strict Flagship Demarcation**: Reserved primary and secondary flagship labels strictly for index 0 and 1, ensuring lower-tier supporting projects (`idx >= 2`) are presented solely as technical breadth and never mislabeled as flagships.
- **Self-Healing Verification Loop**: Deterministic verification pass verifies that both flagship systems are cited in generated letters, triggering an autonomous self-correcting pass if an omission occurs.

### 🔗 Dual-Compatible Markdown Hyperlinks & Auto-Sanitizer

- **Clean Project Name Links**: Prompts instruct Gemini to write clean, natural markdown hyperlinks (`[VeraxAI →](URL)`, `[OpusHunter →](URL)`) rather than verbose boilerplate suffixes like `[VeraxAI Production Release →](URL)`.
- **Automated Spacing Sanitizer**: Built-in regex normalizer (`normalizeGeneratedLetterBody`) automatically repairs accidental spaces between closing brackets and opening parentheses (`[Name  →] (URL)` becomes `[Name →](URL)`), ensuring hyperlinks remain interactive across all markdown viewers and PDF exporters.
- **Universal Multi-User Scaling**: Dynamic evidence formatting automatically calibrates prompts whether a candidate has 2+ flagships, 1 flagship, or 0 codebases (grounding cleanly on verified experience and diplomas).

### 🎛️ AI Formality & Strategy Engine Parity (`index.tsx`, `pipeline.tsx`)

- **5 Distinct Formality Profiles**:
  1. **Professional**: Confident, direct, and universally balanced.
  2. **Technical Deep-Dive**: Written for Engineering Managers and Architects; details exact concurrency models, C++ NDK binaries, Supabase RLS, and schemas.
  3. **Formal Corporate**: Traditional, polished business register for enterprise, banking (SEB, Handelsbanken), and public sector.
  4. **Executive Brief**: Concise, high-signal, outcome-first delivery for time-pressed hiring managers.
  5. **Storytelling**: Engaging narrative journey linking career milestones to system creation.
- **Dual Targeting Engines**: Instant 1-tap selection between **ATS Keyword Mirroring** (exact job-ad vocabulary reuse) and **Achievement Amplifier** (scale, architectural mechanisms, and hard engineering trade-offs).

### 🌐 Universal 100+ Language Engine & Symmetrical 3×3 Quick-Access Selector

- **Symmetrical 3×3 Quick-Access Matrix**: Instant 1-tap switching across your 9 primary regional languages (`profile.tsx` & `cover-letter/[id].tsx`) without UI layout shifts:
  - 🇸🇪 **Svenska (Swedish)** · 🇬🇧 **English (UK)** · 🇺🇸 **English (US)**
  - 🇩🇪 **Deutsch (German)** · 🇳🇴 **Norsk (Norwegian)** · 🇩🇰 **Dansk (Danish)**
  - 🇫🇮 **Suomi (Finnish)** · 🇳🇱 **Nederlands (Dutch)** · 🇫🇷 **Français (French)**
- **1-Tap 100+ Language Search Drawer**: Compact top-right switcher opens an instant, search-indexed catalog of 100+ ISO-standard languages with real-time Supabase cloud synchronization across Android APK and Web.
- **Dialect Detection & Cultural Formality**: Distinguishes regional subtleties (e.g., Riksmål vs. Nynorsk, British vs. American English, formal German Sie vs. du) with culturally authentic sign-offs.

### 📝 Unified Concise Listing Overview Engine (`lib/listingSummary.ts`)

- **Instant Structured Role Briefs**: Synthesizes clean, professional position summaries across Discovery Deck cards (`SwipeDeck.tsx`) and pipeline inspection modals (`index.tsx`, `pipeline.tsx`).
- **No More Vague Placeholders**: Permanently eliminates unhelpful `"The full description is available on the original posting"` texts and raw unformatted key-value dumps.
- **Domain-Aware Synthesis**: Automatically detects technical domains (frontend, backend, systems, mobile, DevOps, legal, healthcare, construction) and highlights key scope and responsibilities even when source boards provide sparse or truncated text.

### 📄 AI CV Refiner & 4-Template 1-Page PDF Studio

- Rewrites and condenses your CV around verified vault evidence without fabricating a single skill.
- Choose between **4 executive A4 templates**:
  1. **Modern Executive** (2-column calibrated navy sidebar, circular photo ring, categorized skill badges)
  2. **Nordic Clean Slate** (Scandinavian 1-column slate banner, 2×2 skill grid, framed project cards)
  3. **Technical Systems** (High-density 1-column engineering layout with monospace stack tags)
  4. **Clean Executive ATS** (Strict linear 1-column parser-first flow with crisp horizontal dividers)
- Export and share as a print-ready A4 PDF directly from Android APK, iOS, or Web.

### 💬 Screening Question Co-Pilot

- Paste employer screening questions or upload a screenshot of an application form.
- OpusHunter drafts honest, copy-ready answers grounded in your profile and evidence ledger — distinguishing direct experience from adjacent experience so you never overclaim.

### 🎯 Deterministic ATS Scoring

- **Real formula**: `60%` verified skill match + `25%` structured requirements (years of experience, education, language, seniority) + `15%` letter articulation.
- **Filler-phrase detection**: Flags clichés in English, Swedish, and other languages so your letter stays sharp and specific.
- **Actionable gap reporting**: Shows matched requirements and missing requirements so you know exactly what proof to upload next.

### 🔬 7-Layer Codebase Architecture Synthesis (`extract-context`)

- Evaluates uploaded `.zip` repositories and Repomix XML archives across **7 deterministic architectural layers**:
  1. **Entry Points & Routing** · 2. **Client State & Caching** · 3. **Business Logic & Services**
  4. **Persistence & Schemas** · 5. **External API Integrations** · 6. **Authentication & Security** · 7. **Test Suites & CI/CD**
- Extracts verified technical proof directly into cover letters and screening answers without manual copy-pasting.

### 🧹 Automated 12-Hour Stale / Taken-Down Listing Purge

- **Background Hygiene Routine**: Automated scheduler executes every 12 hours to verify that listings in the discovery queue remain active on their source job boards.
- **Upstream Liveness Validation**:
  - 🇸🇪 **JobTech**: Queries `GET /ad/{id}` to detect closed postings (`404` or `removed: true`).
  - 🚀 **The Hub**: Queries `/jobs/single/{id}` to detect expired postings (`404` or `closed: true`).
  - 🌐 **SweClockers & Direct Web Feeds**: Executes lightweight HTTP `HEAD` probes detecting `404 Not Found` or `410 Gone`.
- **Zero Data Loss Guarantee**: Un-triaged dead listings are purged from discovery feeds to save candidate time. **Crucially, all jobs with existing candidate applications in `job_applications` are strictly preserved forever** with immutable timestamps, sent emails, and tailored letters.

### 📧 Silent Recruiter Email Discovery

- Searches for official hiring or recruiter emails (`careers@`, `jobb@`, `hr@`, or named contacts) via Hunter.io and job description analysis as soon as you inspect a role.
- Promotes **Send via Gmail / Outlook** to a one-tap primary action when a verified email route is available.

### 🎨 Liquid Neon Aerospace UI & Cross-Platform Parity

- High-contrast frosted obsidian cards (`constants/theme.ts`) designed for zero text bleed-through on OLED phones and desktop monitors.
- Universal `ProfileDropdown` and adaptive navigation shell (`AdaptiveLayout`) with 48px minimum touch targets, safe-area inset math, and 120fps Reanimated transitions.
- Optional tactile micro-electric arcs and concentric cyber rings that respect system reduced-motion preferences.

## 🔒 Privacy, Control & Fair Access

- **Human-in-the-loop always.** Nothing is emailed or marked as applied without your explicit action.
- **Your email, your identity.** Gmail and Outlook are linked via official OAuth 2.0 PKCE (`oauth-link-email`) with automatic token health checks (`check-email-account`).
- **Row-Level Security (RLS).** Every PostgreSQL table and storage bucket enforces `auth.uid() = user_id` isolation.
- **PIN-encrypted BYOK Vault.** Power users can store personal Gemini, RapidAPI, or Hunter.io keys protected by hardware-backed secure storage and PIN verification.
- **Fair Access & Pricing / Limits Transparency**: Limits and quota rules for standard Member and Premium tiers are under ongoing evaluation and will not be finalized until full platform stability is reached. Administrator accounts operate with zero limits. All users currently enjoy generous AI generation access to thoroughly test and explore the system.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is OpusHunter free? What are the limits?</b></summary>
<br/>
The core app is open to test with generous AI generation allowances. Tier limits for member and premium tiers are actively under evaluation and will not be finalized until full platform stability is achieved. Administrator accounts operate with zero limits. Power users can attach their personal Gemini, RapidAPI, or Hunter.io keys via the PIN-encrypted BYOK vault.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How does the 12-hour taken-down listing cleanup work?</b></summary>
<br/>
OpusHunter runs an automated background hygiene routine every 12 hours that verifies live status against upstream providers (JobTech <code>GET /ad/{id}</code>, The Hub <code>closed: true</code>, and direct HTTP HEAD probes for 404/410). Stale or expired postings are automatically removed from the discovery feed. <b>Crucially, any job you have saved or applied to in your pipeline is permanently preserved</b> — your application history, timestamps, and tailored letters are never deleted.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which languages can it write in? How does Quick Access work?</b></summary>
<br/>
Over 100+ native languages are supported via the Google Gemini cascade, with culturally appropriate greetings and formal sign-offs. In your profile and letter editor, a symmetrical 3×3 quick-access matrix gives you instant 1-tap switching between your 9 primary languages (🇸🇪 Swedish, 🇬🇧 English UK, 🇺🇸 English US, 🇩🇪 German, 🇳🇴 Norwegian, 🇩🇰 Danish, 🇫🇮 Finnish, 🇳🇱 Dutch, 🇫🇷 French). A top-right corner switcher opens the full searchable 100+ language drawer with instant cloud sync.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it apply to jobs without asking me?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. Controlled automation, not blind bots. Every application and email requires your explicit confirmation. OpusHunter prepares everything — you review and approve.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the AI make things up about me?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. Letters and screening answers are strictly grounded in your verified evidence ledger (CV, uploaded projects, portfolios, trade licenses, and diplomas). If it isn't in your vault, it isn't in your application.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why isn't it on Google Play yet?</b></summary>
<br/>
The Play Store release is currently in preparation. Until then, the verified APK on the GitHub Releases page is the official direct build.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is OpusHunter only for software developers?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. OpusHunter is built for every profession: builders/construction, legal, finance, healthcare, education, design, engineering, and trades. While software developers can upload code repositories and Repomix archives, any professional can upload portfolios, diplomas, and credentials.
</details>
